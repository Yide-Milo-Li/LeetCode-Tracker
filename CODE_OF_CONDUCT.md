# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Discuss technical work without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or publication of private information.

Give actionable feedback, acknowledge uncertainty, and respect contributors' time and boundaries. Maintainers may moderate or remove disruptive content and restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns to maintainers through an available private channel. Do not disclose personal information in public issues; when no private channel is available, request one without sharing sensitive details.
